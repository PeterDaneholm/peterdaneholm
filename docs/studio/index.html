The approach

Personal in process. Confident in the work.

Studio / 01 is an independent creative production studio working across moving image and still photography. The studio is intentionally flexible: each project starts with its own subject, purpose and audience, rather than a fixed house style.

Sport is one area of interest, alongside commercial projects, portraits, lifestyle and documentary-inspired stories. The aim is to make visual work that feels considered, engaging and true to what it is about.

The studio works hands-on, collaborating with clients and other creatives where a project calls for it. From an initial idea through production and delivery, the focus stays on the story and the details that make it distinct.
